FAQ

Common questions.

What's the difference between BETA, ALPHA, and OMEGA picks?

They're confidence classifications, not bet-sizing advice. BETA is moderate confidence, ALPHA is strong, and OMEGA is our highest-conviction tier.

Does AEE tell me how much to bet?

No. AEE provides classification and analysis only. Bankroll and bet-sizing decisions are always yours.

What sports do you cover?

MLB, NBA, WNBA, NHL, NFL, ATP/WTA tennis, NCAAB, and NCAAF.

Can I cancel or change tiers anytime?

Yes — you can change or cancel your membership at any time from your account settings.

Is sports wagering legal in my state?

Sports wagering legality varies by jurisdiction. It's your responsibility to confirm local legality before acting on any content.

What do I get with VIP that I don't get with Monthly?

VIP adds direct messaging access to our analysts on top of full OMEGA-tier access.
